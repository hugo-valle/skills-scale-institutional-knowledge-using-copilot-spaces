# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead defines quality standards, owns test planning, and ensures release readiness across all project phases. They work closely with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to embed quality throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards, testing strategies, and acceptance criteria
- Plan, coordinate, and oversee all testing activities (unit, integration, regression, UAT)
- Conduct release readiness checks and sign-off before production deployments
- Identify, track, and drive resolution of defects and quality risks
- Collaborate with Developers on test coverage and automation best practices

### Goals
- Ensure software meets quality and reliability standards before release
- Reduce production incidents through proactive testing and risk identification
- Continuously improve testing processes and tooling

### Typical Communication
- Sprint reviews and release readiness meetings
- Defect reports and test summary documentation
- Coordination with Developers on bug triage and fix validation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Architect drives technical design decisions, oversees system integration, and governs the technology stack. They provide guidance to Developers and inform Product Managers and Project Managers on technical feasibility and trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the system architecture and technical design standards
- Evaluate and govern technology stack choices and architectural patterns
- Review and approve significant technical decisions and cross-team integrations
- Identify and mitigate architectural risks and technical debt
- Mentor Developers on design patterns and best practices

### Goals
- Ensure the system is scalable, maintainable, and aligned with business goals
- Reduce technical risk through sound architectural decisions
- Foster engineering consistency across teams and components

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design documentation
- Technical design docs and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Collaboration with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers on feasibility and scope

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst bridges business needs and technical solutions by gathering requirements, facilitating stakeholder communication, and assessing business impact. They work closely with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Developers to ensure delivered solutions meet organizational objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business and functional requirements
- Facilitate workshops and interviews with stakeholders to capture needs
- Translate business requirements into actionable specifications for development teams
- Assess business impact of proposed changes and new features
- Maintain traceability between business requirements and delivered solutions

### Goals
- Ensure solutions align with business objectives and stakeholder expectations
- Reduce rework caused by ambiguous or missing requirements
- Improve communication and shared understanding between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Business requirements documents (BRDs) and user story specifications
- Regular check-ins with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope and priorities

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Representative advocates for the interests of end users or business units throughout the project. They provide timely feedback, validate solutions against real-world needs, and ensure the team remains aligned with user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Represent end-user or business unit perspectives in planning and review sessions
- Provide feedback on prototypes, demos, and delivered features
- Communicate stakeholder concerns and priorities to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT) and validate acceptance criteria
- Escalate issues or risks that affect the business unit to appropriate leads

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions meet the needs of the represented user group or business unit
- Facilitate timely decision-making by providing stakeholder input throughout the project
- Reduce misalignment between team deliverables and real-world use cases

### Typical Communication
- Sprint reviews, demos, and UAT sessions
- Feedback reports and change requests
- Regular briefings with Product Managers and Stakeholder groups

---

## Change Management Lead

### Role Summary
The Change Management Lead manages the people side of change by assessing impact, developing communication plans, and supporting adoption of new processes or systems. They collaborate with Project Managers, Product Managers, and Stakeholder Representatives to ensure transitions are smooth and well-supported.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct change impact assessments and readiness evaluations
- Develop and execute communication plans for organizational changes
- Design and coordinate training and adoption support activities
- Monitor adoption progress and address resistance or barriers
- Maintain alignment between change initiatives and project delivery timelines

### Goals
- Drive successful adoption of changes with minimal disruption to operations
- Ensure stakeholders are informed, prepared, and supported through transitions
- Reduce change-related risks by proactively identifying and addressing resistance

### Typical Communication
- Change impact reports and readiness assessments
- Communication plans and training materials
- Regular coordination with Project Managers and Stakeholder Representatives on adoption status
